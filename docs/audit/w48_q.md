# Wave 48 Q: the critic's list

What was looked at, what was wrong, what was done. Branch `w48-q-polish`.

## 1. The HP bar that "starts low" after a Sanctuary heal

**What the owner sees.** After healing at a Sanctuary, the next battle's creature has full health but its bar
shows less; the numbers are right; the first hit puts the bar right.

**Where it was hunted.** Every path that sets a battle plate's bar (battle_ui/hud.gd `Plate`: `set_creature`,
`_sync_hp`, `tween_hp`, `refresh_stats`, `animate_xp`; battle_scene.gd `intro`, `_sync_hud`, the item path; fx.gd
`_hit`, the `heal` cue, `_switch`), the `Bar` control's draw (it draws straight from `value`, redraws on resize and
on becoming visible), plate caching (none: `Router.battle` instantiates a fresh `battle_scene.tscn` every battle,
and the HUD builds fresh plates in `_ready`), doubles, the follower (no bar), the overworld (no party bar), and the
data between the heal and the fight (nothing outside battle writes `hp`; the owner's own saves have `hp == max_hp()`
for every party member after a Sanctuary visit).

**Finding.** On the plate itself the bar and the numbers are the same two variables (`hp_shown / hp_max`, printed
as "%d / %d"), so they cannot disagree, in 4.6.0 or now. A new frame-driven probe (`tools/dev/hp_probe.gd`, which
ticks real tweens, unlike the synchronous test runner) ran two auto battles around a `heal_party()` with the
owner's own party and never saw the bar differ from the creature's HP, at the intro or after.

The one bar in the game that did show "a lower amount with the right numbers next to it" was the **Summary's HP
row** in 4.6.0: it drew `max_hp / 130` (the stat's size, like the other five rows) beside the text "71 / 71", so a
healed creature read full and looked two-thirds. That was changed on 30 Oct (`73275339`, in 5.0.0) to the real
health bar. The owner's saves and settings are dated 27 Oct (4.6.0), so the report predates that fix; the
installed 5.0.0 app has not yet been played. This session adds the regression test that was missing
(`tests/ui/test_w48_q_critic.gd::test_summary_hp_row_bar_is_current_over_max`) and a plate test that a creature
coming in during a running drain shows full (`test_plate_shows_a_fresh_creature_full_even_mid_drain`).

**A real "bar starts low" bug found on the way, and fixed.** `Forms.apply` set a wild creature's regional form
*after* `Creature.create` had set `hp = max_hp()`; three Isles forms and seven overseas ones lean HP (+4/+6 or
-4), so a Skomer volet, a Guernsey or Kerry moolet and the overseas ones appeared with their old max as current HP:
the foe's plate started visibly part-drained (or the creature sat above its new max). `Forms.apply` now keeps a
full creature full and clamps a hurt one (`test_a_regional_form_keeps_a_full_creature_full`).

If the owner still sees it on 5.0.0+, the probe is the tool: `godot --headless --path . -s
res://tools/dev/hp_probe.gd -- --battle-auto --foe-level=38 --save=<path to a save>` prints every frame where the
plate and the creature disagree.

## 2. The map's route line

`MapLogic.route_points` drew town to town. New `MapLogic.route_legs` (ui/map_logic.gd) types each hop with
`ZoneGraph.hop_mode`: a rail hop follows the drawn railway between the two stations (a small network built from
the map's `rail` polylines, joined where a branch ends against a main line, with stations snapped to the nearest
point on a line), a ferry hop follows its `ferry_paths` crossing in the right direction, and a walk stays straight.
A station with no railway drawn to it (Cairnryan) falls back to a straight leg. ui/world_map.gd uses it. Tests in
`tests/ui/test_w48_q_critic.gd` (synthetic lines and every same-island station pair in the real data).
